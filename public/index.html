How Slook protects each payment

1 · Sign your intent

Your request becomes a signed contract: items, quantity, maximum total, expiry.

2 · Agent proposes

An AI agent chooses products. It holds no keys and cannot move money.

3 · Preflight & authorize

Anything outside your intent is blocked before a PayPal order exists. Otherwise PayPal authorizes.

4 · Verify, then settle

The gate re-fetches the order from PayPal and only then captures, or voids.
